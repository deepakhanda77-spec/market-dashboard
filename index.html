Markets Dashboard

Daily closing charts · Dow Jones, Nifty 50 and 10 Nifty sectoral indices

How to load real data: download each index’s daily history as CSV (Yahoo Finance → Historical Data, or NSE’s index historical page) and use the Upload CSV button on its card (needs a Date and Close column). Or upload one combined CSV with a Date column plus one column per index, named exactly as the card titles. Data stays in your browser. “Simulated sample” is random data for previewing the layout only.
